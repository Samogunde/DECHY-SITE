# 23. Business email, forms, newsletter, analytics, and alerts

Owner direction, 24 October 2026. This records what was built and the steps that need the owner's accounts. Nothing here changes the brand, positioning, or page structure.

## 1. Business email addresses

- Four public addresses, defined once in `src/lib/contact/emails.ts`:
  - hello@dechytech.com: general contact. Contact page, footer, 404 page, privacy policy, terms.
  - sales@dechytech.com: quotes and new projects. Quote and call bands, cost guides, the estimator result.
  - support@dechytech.com: existing clients. Every questions section, the footer, the assistant's fallback messages.
  - billing@dechytech.com: invoices and payments. Terms and the payment question on the cost hub. Never in navigation.
- The business mailbox itself never appears on the site. The validator rejects any other address in content or in JSON-LD.
- Content uses `{{email:sales}}` (or hello, support, billing), which renders the address with a matching mailto link.
- Organization schema: `alternateName: Deki`, `email` (hello@), and two `contactPoint` entries (sales@, support@).

## 2. Forms

| Form | Netlify name | Notification subject | Where |
|---|---|---|---|
| Contact | `contact` | DECHY website: new message from the contact form | Contact page, arrived without a quote link |
| Quote | `quote` | DECHY website: new quote request | Contact page from a quote button or a service page (`?intent=quote` or `?service=`) |
| Estimator lead | `estimator-lead` | DECHY website: new cost estimator lead | Estimator result, "Get an exact quote" |
| Newsletter | `newsletter` | DECHY website: new newsletter signup | End of each article, footer, Resources |

- Every submission goes to our own handler first:
  - honeypot
  - minimum fill time
  - per-visitor rate limit (in memory and in storage, hashed, never the raw address)
  - server-side validation
- An accepted lead is stored for DECHY Admin, then forwarded to Netlify Forms when `NETLIFY_FORMS=on`.
- Netlify Forms sends the email notification, filters spam with Akismet, and keeps a second copy with its own CSV export.
- `public/__forms.html` holds the hidden copies Netlify needs. It is generated by `npx tsx scripts/build-netlify-forms.ts`, and a unit test fails if it is out of date.
- Every lead carries `utm_source`, `utm_medium`, `utm_campaign`, `landing_page`, and `referrer`, saved on the first page of the visit.
- DECHY Admin, Overview, Export: leads and newsletter subscribers as CSV.
- No reply time is promised anywhere. reCAPTCHA is not used at launch; add it only if spam gets through.
- Netlify state (done 24 October 2026): form detection enabled; `NETLIFY_FORMS=on` set for functions and runtime. The forms appear in Netlify after the next deploy.

**Owner step, once, after the next deploy:** in Netlify, open the project, then Forms, then Form notifications. Add an email notification for "Any form" to the business mailbox. Then send one real test through each form and confirm it reaches the mailbox and the Gmail forward and not spam.

## 3. Newsletter

- Email only. Copy: "Cost guides and plain advice on software, websites, apps, and AI. About once a month, nothing else."
- Placed in three quiet places only: the end of each article, one line in the footer, and under the article list on Resources.

## 4. Analytics

### What the site does

- Google Tag Manager `GTM-M7VGXV5C` loads only on www.dechytech.com and dechytech.com: never on localhost, Netlify previews, or dechy-site.netlify.app.
- Consent Mode v2:
  - Analytics storage is denied by default in the EEA, the UK, and Switzerland until the visitor accepts. Elsewhere it is granted unless the visitor declines.
  - Advertising storage is always denied.
  - A one-line choice (Decline, Accept, equal buttons) appears until answered. Cookie settings in the footer reopens it.
- Team traffic: open any page with `?dechy-internal=1` once per browser and analytics stays off in that browser (`?dechy-internal=0` clears it).

- Verified 24 October 2026 on the preview build: no request to googletagmanager.com, google-analytics.com, or clarity.ms, and no data layer. A unit test pins the host rule. The IDs stay the same after the domain is connected. To confirm at launch, check GA4 Realtime.

## 5. Mobile alerts for handoffs and leads

- Today the assistant runs inside the site. Conversations, inquiries, support requests, and newsletter subscribers are stored in Netlify Blobs.
- Every new inquiry and handoff raises an event (`src/lib/server/events.ts`) that is recorded in DECHY Admin and handed to the alert provider.
- **Step 1, Telegram (built):**
  - Alerts go out for:
    - a new contact, quote, or estimator lead
    - a support request
    - an assistant handoff
    - the moment a visitor asks the assistant for a person (before they confirm an email)
    - an inbound business email, once that is connected
  - Each alert shows what happened, the visitor's name, the page, and the reference, with an "Open in DECHY Admin" button.
  - It never carries the email address or the message text.
- **Owner setup, about 5 minutes (the token is a secret, so the owner enters it):**
  1. In Telegram, open @BotFather, send `/newbot`, and name it, for example "DECHY Alerts". Copy the token it gives.
  2. Open the new bot and send it any message (or add it to a team group and send a message there).
  3. Open `https://api.telegram.org/bot<token>/getUpdates` in a browser and copy the `chat` `id` from the reply.
  4. In Netlify, go to the project, then Project configuration, then Environment variables. Add `NOTIFICATION_PROVIDER=telegram`, `TELEGRAM_BOT_TOKEN` (mark it secret), and `TELEGRAM_CHAT_ID`.
  5. After the next deploy, DECHY Admin, Overview shows "Telegram alerts" under Configuration.
- **Step 2, installable DECHY Admin (built):**
  - **App:** `/admin/` has its own web app manifest (name "DECHY Admin", scope `/admin/`, standalone). Install it from Chrome on Android ("Install app"), or on iPhone from Share, then Add to Home Screen.
  - **Phone alerts:**
    - DECHY Admin, Overview, Phone alerts, then "Turn on alerts on this device" subscribes that device to web push. Send a test from the same card.
    - The alert carries the same content as Telegram (what happened, name, page), and tapping it opens the record.
    - The service worker caches nothing.
    - On iPhone, alerts work only from the home screen app (iOS 16.4 or later).
  - **Two-step sign-in:** a six-digit code from an authenticator app, required once `ADMIN_TOTP_SECRET` is set. Each code is accepted once only.
  - **Statuses:** shown as New, Open, Replied, Closed, and Archived. The stored values are unchanged.
  - The inbox, transcripts, notes, assignment, and CSV export already existed.
- **Owner setup for step 2 (no commands, no secret copied by anyone else):**
  1. Open `https://dechy-site.netlify.app/admin/setup/`. Enter your email and a new password (at least 12 characters). The page makes three lines in your browser: `ADMIN_EMAIL`, `ADMIN_PASSWORD_HASH`, and `ADMIN_SESSION_SECRET`. Nothing is sent anywhere.
  2. In Netlify, go to the project, then Project configuration, then Environment variables. Add the three, marking the last two secret, then ask for a redeploy.
  3. Sign in at `/admin/login/`. Open Security and scan the QR code with your authenticator app. Enter the six-digit code to turn two-step sign-in on. The secret stays on the server.
  4. On the phone, install DECHY Admin, open Overview, then Phone alerts, and turn alerts on. The push keys are created by the server the first time.
  5. Lost the phone: set `ADMIN_2FA_RESET=1`, redeploy, sign in, set it up again, then remove the variable.
  6. `npm run admin:totp` and `npm run admin:vapid` remain as optional alternatives, setting the values in Netlify instead.

## 7. Assistant handoff and Telegram live chat (24 October 2026)

Owner direction: no AI provider for now. The assistant answers from the site, and a person takes over through Telegram.

- **Answers from the site.** The starting questions are "What do you build?", "How much does a project cost?", "How long does it take?", and "Talk to a person". Price and timeline answers quote `data/pricing/prices.yaml`, and questions already answered on a page quote that page's answer. Every answer links to the pages it came from (`src/lib/assistant/answers.ts`). Nothing is invented. Price questions are answered, never handed over.
- **When a person is offered.** The visitor asks for one, the assistant misses twice, the visitor sounds frustrated, or the topic is a contract, a complaint, or anything legal. A one-time offer to follow up with a tailored price appears only after two helpful answers.
- **Handoff.** The visitor confirms a short card (name, email, optional note). The record goes to DECHY Admin. The transcript goes to the business mailbox through the Netlify form `assistant-chat`, so the address never appears in the site code. The conversation opens as a live chat in the team's Telegram. No response time is promised.
- **Live chat.** Messages the visitor writes after the handoff go to Telegram with their name and reference. A reply to any of those messages in Telegram (swipe to reply), or a message starting with the reference (`DECHY-ABC123 ...`), appears in the visitor's chat. The panel checks every 5 seconds while open and every 20 seconds while minimized.
  - The webhook is `/api/telegram/`. Telegram sends a secret header derived from the bot token, and replies are accepted from `TELEGRAM_CHAT_ID` only.
  - Live chat carries message text to Telegram: that is its purpose, and the visitor is told a person reads it. Lead alerts still carry the name, the page, and a link only.
- **Switching it on.** After the deploy, open DECHY Admin, then Security, then Telegram live chat, and press "Connect Telegram replies". This points the bot's webhook at the site. No new environment variable is needed.
- **Cost.** No paid tools. Credits stay small: about 0.05 credits for a ten-minute live chat, from polling. Netlify Forms submissions are free.
