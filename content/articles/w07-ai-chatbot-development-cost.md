The range is wide because "chatbot" covers very different products. This guide breaks down what you pay for, so you can budget with confidence.

## Three ways to get a chatbot

**1. A ready-made chatbot platform.**
You sign up, upload your content, customize the look and paste a code snippet on your site.
- Setup cost: $0 to a few hundred dollars
- Monthly cost: about $0 to $300, depending on usage and features
- Best for: testing the idea, small sites, simple question and answer

**2. A configured platform with professional setup.**
A specialist sets up a ready-made tool properly: structuring your content, writing rules, adding integrations and testing.
- Cost: about {{fact:price.ai-ai-assistant-on-your-content}} setup, plus the monthly platform fee
- Best for: businesses that want a reliable result without a full custom build

**3. A custom AI assistant.**
Built from scratch around your data, systems and rules, with your own design and logic.
- Cost: about {{fact:price.ai-ai-feature-in-your-product-from}} to {{fact:price.ai-ai-agent-connected-to-your-systems-to}} or more
- Best for: customer-facing chatbots with real business impact, heavy integrations, strict privacy needs or a need to own the technology

## What affects the price

**Knowledge sources.** A bot trained on a few FAQ pages is simple. One that searches thousands of documents, product data and policies needs more engineering and more testing.

**Integrations.** Connecting to your CRM (customer relationship management system), calendar, helpdesk, ecommerce store or database takes time. Each integration is a mini project.

**Actions.** Answering questions is simpler than doing things. Booking appointments, creating tickets, checking order status or processing returns require careful logic and security.

**Accuracy and safety controls.** Good chatbots include rules about what to answer, what to decline, how to cite sources and when to hand over. These safeguards take design and testing.

**Human handoff.** Real-time alerts, transcript passing and agent dashboards add cost, and they are worth it.

**Channels.** A website chat is one thing. Adding WhatsApp, Instagram, Messenger or SMS multiplies the setup.

**Languages.** Multi-language support needs extra content work and testing.

**Design.** A branded, polished chat window takes more design time than a standard widget.

**Compliance.** Handling health, finance or personal data adds requirements for logging, consent, data location and review.

## Running costs

The build is not the full cost. Budget for:

- **Platform subscription:** if you use a ready-made tool, $0 to $300 a month, with higher tiers for more chats
- **AI usage:** custom bots pay the AI provider per use. For small and mid-size business traffic this is often $20 to $300 a month, but it grows with conversation volume.
- **Hosting:** typically $10 to $100 a month for a custom bot
- **Maintenance:** updating content, adjusting rules and fixing issues. Plan for 15 to 25 percent of the build cost per year, or a small monthly retainer.
- **Review time:** someone should read conversations weekly, especially in the first months

## What you get back

A chatbot pays for itself in one of three ways.

- **Saved time:** if it handles 30 to 50 percent of routine questions, your team gets hours back each week.
- **More leads:** answering visitors instantly, at any hour, captures inquiries that would otherwise be lost.
- **Better service:** faster answers and clean handoffs improve how customers feel about you.

To estimate the return, count how many routine questions you get each week, multiply by the minutes each takes and put a value on that time. Then add the value of leads you currently miss out of hours.

## How to keep the cost down

1. **Start narrow.** Launch with one job, like answering your top 30 questions, and expand later.
2. **Clean your content first.** Clear, current information makes a bot better and cheaper to set up.
3. **Use a ready-made platform at the start**, then go custom when you hit its limits.
4. **Skip the extra channels** until the website version works.
5. **Plan the handoff early.** Fixing a bot that traps visitors is more expensive than designing the exit from the start.

## Hidden costs to watch for

- Per-conversation fees that grow with success
- Extra charges for removing the vendor's branding
- Limits on stored conversations or knowledge size
- Paid add-ons for integrations you assumed were included
- Consultants needed later to fix poor setup

Ask for a cost estimate at 1,000, 5,000 and 20,000 conversations per month. It exposes pricing surprises fast.

## Frequently asked questions

### How much does a simple AI chatbot cost?
A ready-made tool can start free or at a few tens of dollars a month. With professional setup and testing, a simple FAQ bot typically costs {{fact:price.ai-ai-assistant-on-your-content}}.

### How much does a custom AI chatbot cost?
Custom builds usually run from about {{fact:price.ai-ai-feature-in-your-product-from}} to {{fact:price.ai-ai-agent-connected-to-your-systems-to}} or more, depending on integrations, safeguards and channels.

### How long does it take to build a chatbot?
A configured ready-made tool can be live in one to two weeks. A custom assistant usually takes four to ten weeks, including testing.

### Are AI chatbots expensive to run?
For small and mid-size business traffic, running costs are usually modest: platform or hosting fees plus AI usage. Costs rise with volume.

### Is a chatbot worth it for a small business?
If you answer the same questions daily or miss inquiries after hours, yes. Start small and measure the time saved and leads captured.

### Can I build a chatbot myself?
Yes, with a ready-made platform for simple needs. For accuracy, integrations and safety, professional help saves time and avoids mistakes.
