# Certification badges

Supplied by the owner on 21 October 2026 (folder "Certifications"), as the professional certifications held by members of the DECHY team.

- Each badge is the issuer's own artwork, shown by its holder's company to state the certification.
- `scripts/prepare-certification-badges.mjs` removed only plain white or gray backgrounds and trimmed the margins. The artwork was not redrawn, recolored, or altered.
- The original file name of each badge is mapped to its published name in that script.
- One duplicate was supplied: a smaller copy of the AWS Generative AI Developer badge. It was not used.
- Copy presents these as certifications held by the team, never as a company-level partner status.
